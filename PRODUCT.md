# Planet Studio

## Register

product

## Users

People creating a miniature planet for their own webpage, without placing
every tree or building manually.

## Product Purpose

Paint regions on a blank globe, automatically arrange decorations, and export
an interactive offline HTML file. JSON preserves editable work.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, calm. Preserve the established low-poly art and compact
mint/coral editing interface rather than adding a marketing landing page.

## Anti-references

No copied models, decorative dashboard cards, fake projects, or controls that
only look functional. This tool remains separate from the author's homepage.

## Design Principles

- The full-bleed globe is the primary editing surface.
- A brush populates an area, not one manually placed object.
- Environment changes preserve the composition.
- Saved projects and standalone exports must reproduce the same world.
- Mobile tools remain reachable without covering the globe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard editing, named controls, visible focus, reduced motion,
pointer cancellation, and responsive layouts. No additional needs specified.
